Estimated inclusion moisture— %

The starter contribution is subtracted from the flour and water you add to the bowl. Chaos is built at 1:6:10 (seed:water:flour); the build itself is treated as approximately 60% hydration for dough math.

Tip: if your starter is 100% hydration, 100 g starter contains approximately 50 g flour and 50 g water.
